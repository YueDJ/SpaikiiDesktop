## 自动故障转移

### 健康检查配置
`yaml
failover:
  health_check:
    interval: "10s"
    timeout: "5s"
    unhealthy_threshold: 3
    healthy_threshold: 2
`

### 转移流程
`mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    participant HC as 健康检查
    participant Primary as 主节点
    participant Secondary as 备用节点
    participant FC as 故障转移控制器
    HC->>Primary: 健康检查失败 (3次)
    HC->>FC: 报告主节点不健康
    FC->>Secondary: 提升为主节点
    FC->>FC: 更新路由配置
    FC->>Notify: 发送告警通知
    Note over Primary,Secondary: 故障转移完成
`

## 服务降级策略

### 性能降级
- 减少并发请求处理
- 延长超时时间
- 禁用非关键功能

### 功能降级
- 禁用实时功能
- 使用缓存数据
- 返回预设响应

### 优雅降级配置
`yaml
degradation:
  levels:
    - name: "normal"
      max_concurrency: 100
      timeout: "30s"
      features: ["all"]
    - name: "degraded"
      max_concurrency: 50
      timeout: "60s"
      features: ["chat", "tools"]
    - name: "minimal"
      max_concurrency: 10
      timeout: "120s"
      features: ["chat"]
`

async def auto_recovery():

            break
